01 / About

WHO I AM

who i am

I build the parts of a product that people actually touch. The interface, the flow behind it, and the logic that makes the whole thing hold together.

I finished a B.Sc. in Software Engineering at Kinneret Academic College in 2026. Across six projects I wrote user-facing applications in Angular, Android and Unity, and worked server-side in Java, Node.js and Python - usually owning a feature from the database schema up to the screen.

Before the degree I spent two years in quality assurance on medical ventilators. That is where I learned to ask what breaks this before asking what ships, and it is the reason I was trusted with the Quality Owner role on my final year team.

I am looking for a junior developer role, ideally in front-end or application development, and I am genuinely open to back-end, mobile and QA as well. What matters most to me is joining a team that reviews code and explains its reasoning. Further along I intend to take an M.Sc. in a field that builds on this, alongside working.
